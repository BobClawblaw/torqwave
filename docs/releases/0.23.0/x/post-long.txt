Torqwave 0.23.0 is out.

Torqwave is a free, open-source audio editor and DAW in one program for macOS, Windows and Linux — spectral editing, repair and mastering alongside multitrack sessions, instruments, a sequencer, automation, surround and video. No account, no subscription.

0.23.0 lifts Torqwave from 45 to 47 of 53 rows in our documented comparison against 15 editors and DAWs (+Audio to MIDI, CLAP) and grows the built-in plug-in suite from 22 to 32.

HERE IS EVERYTHING NEW IN 0.23.0

SIX NEW LIVE CREATIVE FX
Real-time built-in effects for tracks, buses and the Effects Rack:
• Delay — Stereo, Ping-Pong and Tape modes with tempo sync, feedback HP/LP filters, tape wow/drive and input-triggered wet ducking.
• Distortion — Tape, Tube, Soft Clip, Hard Clip, Wavefold and Bitcrush/Downsample with 4x oversampling (latency-compensated) plus pre-HPF / post-LPF tone shaping.
• Auto-Filter — LP12/24, BP, HP, Notch and Comb driven by a tempo-synced LFO (with stereo phase), audio envelope follower and sidechain key.
• Volume Shaper — tempo-synced sidechain pumping and gating (Club Pump, Tight Duck, Deep Duck, Trance Gate, Half-Time, Saw and custom 8-step curve) with a low-band split crossover.
• Noise Gate — hysteresis, hold, floor range, sidechain filtering/key and linked surround channels.
• Tremolo & Auto-Pan — tempo-synced across 6 shapes with 0–180° stereo phase spread.

FOUR LIVE MIDI FX & PIANO ROLL UPGRADES
• Live MIDI FX chain — MIDI Arpeggiator, Chord Trigger (triads/7ths/9ths/sus/diatonic + inversions + strum), Scale Lock (snap/filter/transpose, stuck-note safe) and Note Echo, running directly ahead of any instrument on the same track.
• Piano Roll Ratchets (1x–8x rhythmic subdivisions) and Micro-Timing offsets per note, saved in sessions.
• Built-in Groove & Swing templates (MPC 54%–66%, 16th Shuffle, Push, Laid-Back, plus extracted audio grooves).
• Cross-track Ghost Notes showing overlapping notes from other instrument tracks behind your active clip.

TORQWAVE SYNTH UPGRADES (100% BIT-IDENTICAL ON OLD PATCHES)
• LFOs 3 & 4, 24 modulation slots and Slot Depth as a modulation destination.
• 16-node smooth Catmull-Rom MSEG "Curve" LFO shape you can edit directly in the LFO display.
• Built-in tempo-synced Arpeggiator (Up, Down, Up-Down, Down-Up, As Played, Random, 1–4 octaves, gate, swing).
• 4-Scene continuous "Evolve" morphing with state persistence.
• Optional 9-oscillator Complex matching in Decrypt.
• Plus 58 new hand-crafted 9-oscillator musical club patches in Complex (116 Complex patches + 3,569 factory patches total).
• All 3,627 prior patches, 1,024 presets, 74 reference renders and 3 saved sessions remain sample-for-sample identical to 0.22.0.

AUDIO TO MIDI (PURE DSP)
Right-click any audio clip in a session:
• Convert to MIDI (Melody) — YIN pitch tracking with RMS velocity and optional scale quantization.
• Convert to MIDI (Drums) — 3-band transient classification into Kick (36), Snare (38), Closed Hat (42) and Open Hat (46), plus groove offset extraction.

SAMPLER & BASIC DRUMS DEPTH
• Torqwave Sampler now imports standard SFZ multisamples, cycles per-zone Round-Robin groups (1–16), and supports Signalsmith Stretch tempo-synced Warp playback.
• Basic Drums adds 8 stereo Aux output buses for per-pad multi-out routing (Main + Aux 1–8), saved with your kit and session.

TRACK MODULATORS, CLAP 1.2 & HEADLESS CI
• Track-Level Modulators — attach tempo-synced/free LFOs and audio envelope followers to any session track to modulate any automatable parameter on any plug-in.
• Native CLAP 1.2 plug-in hosting across macOS, Windows and Linux (scanned out of process alongside VST3, Audio Units and LV2).
• Automated headless test suite (TorqwaveTests --headless) now running in CI on Windows and Linux, alongside 2,782 passing checks on Apple Silicon and Intel (Rosetta).

WHAT IT STILL DOESN'T DO
No Dolby Atmos, no ARA 2, no notation editor, no full neural stem export, and installers aren't notarized with a Developer ID yet (so macOS asks on first launch). Our 15-product feature comparison lists every gap as plainly as what's built.
