Basic Drums and Torqwave Sampler

Basic Drums' 7 kits, each on two beats, and Torqwave Sampler's 11 factory presets.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing, except that a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. They play the audition phrases Torqwave itself plays when you choose a sound, at 125 BPM. 1.5 MB in all.

Basic Drums

A drum machine with 30 sounds that cover every General MIDI drum note, each with its own tune, decay, tone, level, pan and drive, and a filter, crush, reverb and delay on every pad. A kit sets all of it at once.
