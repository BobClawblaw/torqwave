Basic Drums and Torqwave Sampler

Basic Drums' 12 kits, each playing factory grooves written for it, and Torqwave Sampler's 11 factory presets.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing, except that a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Each kit plays the Sequence Library's factory grooves written for it (of its Drums › Full Grooves, the three with the most energy, or all of them where a kit has fewer), as the library's preview plays them, at the tempo they were written at. The sampler's presets play the audition phrases Torqwave plays when you choose a sound, at 125 BPM. 5.3 MB in all.

Basic Drums
