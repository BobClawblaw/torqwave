Basic Drums and Torqwave Sampler

Basic Drums' 15 kits, each playing grooves written for it, the 24 synthesized sources any of its pads can play, and Torqwave Sampler's 11 factory presets.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing. Dead air is cut: a sound slow to arrive (a pad, a drone, a swell, a riser) is joined partway in, 30 ms before it reaches a quarter of its peak, with a fade in, and its card says how far in; silence after a sound has died away is left off; and a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Each kit plays the Sequence Library's factory grooves written for it (of its Drums › Full Grooves, the three with the most energy, or all of them where a kit has fewer), as the library's preview plays them, at the tempo they were written at. A kit no factory groove is written for yet plays two of the audition beats and a four-bar groove written for it, with a fill in its last bar. Each source plays alone and in a groove; the nothing between its hits is cut to a quarter of a second. The sampler's presets play the audition phrases Torqwave plays when you choose a sound, at 125 BPM. 10.0 MB in all.

Basic Drums

A drum machine with 64 pads. The first 30 are sounds that cover every General MIDI drum note, 21 of them with a Modern type beside the Classic one; the other 34 are user pads on notes of their own. Any pad can play one of 24 more synthesized sources, or your own samples in up to 16 layers, by velocity and taking turns. Each pad has its own tune, decay, tone, level, pan and drive, a filter, crush, reverb and delay, and five effects of its own: an envelope, a transient shaper, a saturator, an EQ and a compressor. A kit sets all of it at once.

Torqwave ships no samples: everything on this page is synthesized, and the factory kits use no sample. A pad plays the sounds you give it, read where they are.
