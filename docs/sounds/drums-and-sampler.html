Basic Drums and Torqwave Sampler

Basic Drums' 7 kits, each playing factory grooves written for it, and Torqwave Sampler's 11 factory presets.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing, except that a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Each kit plays the Sequence Library's factory grooves written for it (of its Drums › Full Grooves, the three with the most energy), as the library's preview plays them, at the tempo they were written at; a kit no factory groove uses, and the sampler's presets, play the audition phrases Torqwave plays when you choose a sound, at 125 BPM. 3.3 MB in all.

Basic Drums

A drum machine with 30 sounds that cover every General MIDI drum note, each with its own tune, decay, tone, level, pan and drive, and a filter, crush, reverb and delay on every pad. A kit sets all of it at once.
