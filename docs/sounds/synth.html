Torqwave Synth: 750 of its patches

A wavetable synth with 3,743 factory patches in 29 categories. Here are 750 of them: the ones that use the most of the synth, from every category, and 18 chosen by ear. Under each name is what the patch is made of, read off the synth with the patch loaded.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing. Dead air is cut: a sound slow to arrive (a pad, a drone, a swell, a riser) is joined partway in, 30 ms before it reaches a quarter of its peak, with a fade in, and its card says how far in; silence after a sound has died away is left off; and a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Each patch plays one of the audition phrases Torqwave itself plays when you choose a sound, at 125 BPM; the 18 chosen by ear play a part that suits them. 61.2 MB in all; a clip loads only when you play it.

How they were chosen

  • By count, not by ear. Every patch was loaded and its parts in use counted. A point for each oscillator on, each of those with unison voices, each with a warp, the sub, the noise, each filter, each effect on, each modulation routing in use and each named macro. Each card gives its count.
  • From every category. Each category has its share of the 750 (about a fifth of its patches, ten at least), and within it the patches with the highest count come first, the earlier in the synth's list among equals.
  • A high count is not a promise of a better sound. It finds the patches with layers, movement and effects; a plain sub with one oscillator is a good sub and counts low. Apart from the 18 chosen by ear, these were not listened to one by one.
