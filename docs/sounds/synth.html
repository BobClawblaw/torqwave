Torqwave Synth: 163 of its patches

A wavetable synth with 3,743 factory patches in 29 categories. Here are 163 of them, from every category: in each, the first patch in the list and four more spread evenly through it (chosen by their place, not by ear, so this is what the list is like), and 18 chosen by ear for the first version of this page. Under each name is what the patch is made of, read off the synth with the patch loaded.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing, except that a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Patches chosen by their place play one of the audition phrases Torqwave itself plays when you choose a sound, at 125 BPM. 14.4 MB in all.
