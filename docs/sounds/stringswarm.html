StringSwarm: 38 of its presets

A guitar with every string tuned to the note you play, through a fuzz or a crunch amp: one note becomes a wall of strings beating against each other. With its source switched to Swarm, an oscillator for each string takes the strings' place, fanned out, converging, gliding and drifting. It has 296 factory presets in 11 categories; here are 38: 22 chosen by ear, and from each category the first preset and two more spread through the list.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing. Dead air is cut: a sound slow to arrive (a pad, a drone, a swell, a riser) is joined partway in, 30 ms before it reaches a quarter of its peak, with a fade in, and its card says how far in; silence after a sound has died away is left off; and a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. 4.4 MB in all.
