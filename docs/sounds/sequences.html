The Sequence Library: 118 of its 1,032 factory sequences

The Sequence Library ships 1,032 sequence presets in 37 styles: drums, bass lines, harmony, pads, arps, melodies and effects, each with a line saying what it does. Here are 118 on their own: one of each role from nine techno and industrial styles, and a bass line and a melody from each of the others. Of each role the one with the most energy was taken (the first by name among equals): chosen by rule, not by ear. The kits play six sequences together.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing, except that a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Each plays on the voices it comes with, Torqwave Synth patches and Basic Drums kits, at the tempo it was written at, as the Sequence Library's own preview plays it; a loop under five seconds goes round again. 12.3 MB in all.
