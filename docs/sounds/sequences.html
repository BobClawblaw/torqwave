The Sequence Library: 119 of its 1,032 factory sequences

The Sequence Library ships 1,032 sequence presets in 37 styles: drums, bass lines, harmony, pads, arps, melodies and effects, each with a line saying what it does. Here are 119 on their own: one of each role from nine techno and industrial styles, and from each of the others a bass line and a melody, an arp or a harmony part. Of each, the one that does the most by measure was taken: its notes to a bar, its different pitches and how varied its rhythm is, on a voice the style's other clips do not play. A part with two notes a bar or fewer, or that repeats one note, is passed over, and where a style's bass lines all do that, another role stands in. A sub on one note is shown nowhere. Club tools has only one-sound parts, and shows its kick and its hats. Chosen by that rule, not by ear: nobody has listened to them one by one. The kits play six sequences together.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing, except that a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Each plays on the voices it comes with, Torqwave Synth patches and Basic Drums kits, at the tempo it was written at, as the Sequence Library's own preview plays it; a loop under five seconds goes round again. 13.9 MB in all.
