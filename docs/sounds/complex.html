Torqwave Synth: all 116 Complex patches

Multi-oscillator patches built for Torqwave Synth's nine-oscillator engine, four in each of the 29 categories (87 of the 116 use all nine oscillators): mono low end below 120 Hz, parallel stereo width and air, tempo-synced movement as notes are held, and expressive velocity response. 0.22.1 adds two new nine-oscillator musical club patches to every category alongside the initial 58 from 0.22.0.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing. Dead air is cut: a sound slow to arrive (a pad, a drone, a swell, a riser) is joined partway in, 30 ms before it reaches a quarter of its peak, with a fade in, and its card says how far in; silence after a sound has died away is left off; and a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Each patch plays four bars at 132 BPM in its category's phrase. 17.1 MB in all; a clip loads only when you play it.
