New in Torqwave 0.23.0: 8 audio showcase demos

Hear the new 0.23.0 engine features in action: the six Live Creative FX (Tape Delay with wet ducking, 4x-oversampled Distortion, Auto-Filter, Volume Shaper / Club Pump, Tremolo & Auto-Pan), the four Live MIDI FX chained ahead of Torqwave Synth, the Synth's built-in tempo-synced Arpeggiator and 4-Scene continuous Evolve morphing, Piano Roll Ratchets & MPC 62% Groove Templates, Audio to MIDI melody extraction, and Track-Level LFO Modulators.

Each clip was rendered directly by Torqwave 0.23.0 (TorqwaveTests --023-demos) and encoded as a 48 kHz LAME V4 MP3 (1.4 MB total).

Torqwave Synth · 0.23.0

Built-in tempo-synced Arpeggiator (1/16 Up-Down, 2 octaves, 58% swing) while 4-Scene Evolve morphs continuously across Velvet Roller, Strobe Pluck, Palazzo Chord and Elysian Bloom.

Rendered directly by Torqwave 0.23.0 (TorqwaveTests --023-demos): 8 bars at 128 BPM.
