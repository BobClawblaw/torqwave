Hear Torqwave: 1243 clips of what is built in

Torqwave comes with four instruments, thousands of patches and presets for them (including 116 multi-oscillator Complex patches), and a library of 1,024 sequences and 116 kits to play on them. These pages let you hear them before you download anything.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing. Dead air is cut: a sound slow to arrive (a pad, a drone, a swell, a riser) is joined partway in, 30 ms before it reaches a quarter of its peak, with a fade in, and its card says how far in; silence after a sound has died away is left off; and a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click.

How they were chosen and made

  • Chosen by ear: 18 Torqwave Synth patches and 22 StringSwarm presets, picked for the first version of this page and each given a part that suits it (four bars at 120 BPM). Their cards say so.
  • Chosen by rule: everything else, so the pages show what the lists are like and not only their best. Of the synth's patches, the ones that use the most of the synth, by a count of their parts in use, a share from every category; from each StringSwarm category, the first preset and two more; every Basic Drums kit and each of its 24 sources, every sampler preset and every kit of the Sequence Library; and of the sequences, only figures that one style alone has, each once, the one that does the most by measure where there are several.
  • Played as the app plays them: patches and presets on the audition phrases Torqwave plays when you choose a sound; kits and sequences through the Sequence Library's own preview, at the tempo they were written at.
  • Not listened to one by one. The 40 chosen by ear were; the rest were rendered and checked by measurement only (each has sound, none reaches full scale). If one is wrong, say so.
  • MP3s at a variable bit rate (LAME V4), stereo, 48 kHz, written by Torqwave's own exporter. The app plays the instruments themselves, not these files.
