Torqwave Synth: Bass

54 of the 271 factory patches in Bass: the ones that use the most of the synth, the highest count first.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing. Dead air is cut: a sound slow to arrive (a pad, a drone, a swell, a riser) is joined partway in, 30 ms before it reaches a quarter of its peak, with a fade in, and its card says how far in; silence after a sound has died away is left off; and a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. A point for each oscillator on, each of those with unison voices, each with a warp, the sub, the noise, each filter, each effect on, each modulation routing in use and each named macro. 2.6 MB in all; a clip loads only when you play it.
