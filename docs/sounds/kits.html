The Sequence Library: all 116 factory kits

A kit is a set of sequences written to play together: a groove, a bass line, chords or stabs, an arp or a hook, a fill and a transition. Dropped into a session it makes a track for each part, with its instrument. The Sequence Library ships 116 of them in 36 styles, techno and industrial first. This is every one, each part named with the voice it plays on.

Each clip was rendered by Torqwave's own code (TorqwaveTests --sound-demos, in the repository) and written as an MP3. Nothing was added: no effects, no levelling, no editing. Dead air is cut: a sound slow to arrive (a pad, a drone, a swell, a riser) is joined partway in, 30 ms before it reaches a quarter of its peak, with a fade in, and its card says how far in; silence after a sound has died away is left off; and a clip still sounding where it stops is faded over its last 30 ms so the cut does not click. Each plays on the voices it comes with, Torqwave Synth patches and Basic Drums kits, at the tempo it was written at, as the Sequence Library's own preview plays it; a loop under five seconds goes round again. 17.3 MB in all.
