# Styles

Every factory preset in the [Sequence Library](07-instruments-and-midi.md#presets) carries a style, and it is written to that style's conventions. This page says what those conventions are, so the presets can teach as well as play: open one in the [Sequencer](07-instruments-and-midi.md#the-sequencer) next to the row here and you can see each rule in its notes. **Filters > Style** in the library shows a style's presets, and **By Style** groups them (see [Sort, group and filter](07-instruments-and-midi.md#sort-group-and-filter)).

A style is named for what it plays, not for a scene: the presets are figures and voices you build from, and a scene's name would promise a record's sound. No two presets of a sub-role play the same notes, and a figure is in three styles at most, so a style's presets are its own and not another's under a new name. There are 37 styles in ten families. The presets lean towards the four-on-the-floor styles of **Floor** (398 of the 1,024) and the sequenced, metallic styles of **Machine** (321): Dark Floor, Driving, Peak-time and Machine Pulse have the most, 44 to 48 each. Around them are smaller sets of 17 or 18 for Machine Funk, Fast Grind, Fast Flow, Dark Space, Drone, Deep Swing, Neon Menace and Rolling Spiral, 22 for Broken Beat, and six game styles of 22 to 24.

## Floor

Four on the floor, for a club.

| Style | Tempo | What defines it |
|---|---|---|
| Floor › Dark Floor | 128–135 | A short kick with a long rumble on the 16ths after it, offbeat hats under 70 % velocity, a ride in 8ths with every second hit quieter, a rolling bass on the root, one-note minor stabs |
| Floor › Driving | 130–138 | Kick-bass-bass-bass rolling 16ths, a clap on 2 and 4 layered with a snare, 16th hats shaped by velocity, ghost kicks before 2 and 4 |
| Floor › Peak-time | 130–138 | The driving groove at full weight: call-and-response riffs, stabs and builds for the top of a set |
| Floor › Percussive | 132–142 | Loop tracks built from percussion: tom, conga and metal figures in 16ths with shifting accents, a rolling kick and bass, little or no harmony |
| Floor › Hypnotic | 128–134 | Percussion in 3- and 5-step cycles against the bar, very few pitches, 4- and 8-bar loops that change slowly |
| Floor › Minimal | 122–130 | Sparse clicks and rims, a short syncopated bass, one echoed chord |
| Floor › Club tools | 125–132 | Kick only, kick and hat, top loops and percussion loops: parts built for layering |
| Floor › Melodic | 120–126 | A rolling 16th bass on the chord roots, i–♭VI–♭III–♭VII and i–iv–♭VI–♭VII progressions, 16th arps over the chords, a 1-bar lead restated over 8 |
| Floor › Factory Floor | 126–130 | An overdriven kick, low tom figures in the kick's gaps, a noise snare, metallic percussion |
| Floor › Hard Floor | 145–155 | An offbeat rumble, metallic 16th percussion with accents every 3 and 6 steps, kick rolls |
| Floor › Squelch | 128–140 | Resonant one-oscillator lines: 16 steps, the root with octave jumps, rests, accents on a quarter to two fifths of the steps, slides on a tenth to a quarter, minor pentatonic or Phrygian |
| Floor › Echo Chamber | 118–122 | A soft kick with a rumble, a rim on 2 and 4, pedal and offbeat closed hats, a sub bass in half notes or on the offbeat, one minor chord held and echoed |

## Machine

Sequenced basses, drum machines and metal.

| Style | Tempo | What defines it |
|---|---|---|
| Machine › Machine Pulse | 110–130 | A sequencer bass driven in hard, short 16ths on a few notes (the root, its octave and fifth, the flat seventh, a semitone above), accented and syncopated, calling low and answering itself higher; militant drums, a hard snare and struck metal; cold minor and bare fifths, the chord barely moving (one step from i to ♭II, ♭VI or ♭VII and back at most); alarm lines a semitone or a tritone apart. Its bare kind stands on one bass note, a kick, a snare and one metal sound |
| Machine › Broken Circuit | 100–135 | Syncopated kicks, flammed snares, a 16th bass with a semitone shift, Phrygian and harmonic minor clusters, pitch-bend dives |
| Machine › Iron Riff | 90–140 | Programmed rock beats, synth riffs doubling a guitar in 8ths with ♭2 and ♭5 moves, power chords |
| Machine › Bright Pulse | 125–140 | Four-on-the-floor drums with offbeat hats and an offbeat bass, minor 4-chord loops, gated 16th arps, 2-bar call-and-answer leads |
| Machine › Overdrive | 130–150 | A hard 4/4, snare-roll fills, a distorted offbeat bass, 16th supersaw riffs |
| Machine › Night Pulse | 120–135 | Machine Pulse's bass sequences under pop chords and a hook to sing: a driven 8th or 16th bass climbing root, fifth, flat seventh and octave, minor progressions, square and saw leads in 2-bar phrases |
| Machine › Night Wave | 100–118 | Drum-machine pop beats with long-reverb snares, an 8th root bass, chorused minor pads, simple 2-bar melodies |
| Machine › Machine Drive | 125–140 | A four-on-the-floor kick and hats with Machine Pulse's 16th bass hammering, gating and biting in the gaps, struck metal, bare fifths and the flat seventh, the root changing in bar 3 or 4 |
| Machine › Dark Circuit | 110–125 | Broken and marching kicks, a gated snare, flat 8th and 16th hats, a galloping or 16th bass on a reese, harmonic minor and Phrygian, brass stabs over noise and drones |
| Machine › Cold Glow | 104–120 | A drum machine at half weight with a snare or rim on 2 and 4, an 8th bass on the root, glassy chords and plucks, a sparse lead |

## Around them

| Style | Tempo | What defines it |
|---|---|---|
| Funk › Machine Funk | 124–130 | A syncopated machine kick in place of four on the floor, a snare or clap on 2 and 4, 16th hats, a cowbell, a bass that jumps with the kick |
| Broken › Broken Beat | 128–135 | A broken kick and a snare with ghost notes around it, 8th or shuffled hats, a reese or rolling bass, sometimes at half time |
| Fast › Fast Grind | 172–174 | The two-step break (kick on 1 and on the and of 3, snare on 2 and 4) with ghost snares and kick rolls, a reese bass that holds or moves in half time, Phrygian stabs |
| Fast › Fast Flow | 170–174 | The same break played lighter, with a shaker, a sub or warm bass in half time, and i–♭VI–♭III–♭VII on warm chords and organ |
| Swing › Deep Swing | 118–122 | Four on the floor with a clap on 2 and 4, offbeat open hats, shaker and conga, a deep bass that walks between chord tones, organ and warm chords |
| Neon › Neon Menace | 100–124 | A hard four-on-the-floor or rock kick, a gated snare, a 16th bass in octaves, brass and saw chords over i–♭VI–♭VII–i or i–♭VI–iv–V |
| Spiral › Rolling Spiral | 140–145 | A kick on every beat with the bass on the three 16ths after it, offbeat hats, one root for bars at a time, acid and saw lines in Phrygian or harmonic minor |
| Space › Dark Space | 70–90 | A low boom now and then in place of a beat, a drone or sub under it, slow Phrygian and harmonic minor pads, glass and bell notes far apart |
| Space › Drone | 60–80 | Almost no drums; one root held for the whole loop, warm and droning pads, a few glass notes |

## Game audio

Loops for the moments a game has. Each game style also has **UI Sounds** and **Stingers** under SFX: short figures for a menu move, a pick-up, a win or a loss.

| Style | Tempo | What defines it |
|---|---|---|
| Game › Combat | 140–160 | Marching and galloping kicks, a gated snare, tom figures and crashes, a 16th or galloping bass, brass and saw riffs in minor |
| Game › Boss | 160–175 | Combat at full weight and faster: galloping and drum and bass kicks, flat 16th hats, toms and metal, harmonic minor and Phrygian brass |
| Game › Exploration | 90–110 | A half-time kick, a rim, shuffled hats, conga and shaker, a walking or pulsing bass, warm chords and glass arps on i–♭VI–♭III–♭VII |
| Game › Menu | 90–100 | Very little: a sparse kick and hat, a pulsing sub, glass and warm chords, a bell melody that can loop for minutes |
| Game › 8-bit | 140–160 | Thin, crushed sounds on every part: a bass in 8ths and octaves, fast arps standing in for chords, a whistling lead, and the Electro kit of Basic Drums with a snare on 2 and 4 |
| Game › Dread | 70–90 | Sparse booms and low toms, metal hits, a drone or slow pulse, harmonic minor and Phrygian chords over noise |

## What every preset keeps

- **On the 16th grid**, 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars long. A 4-bar preset changes in its last bar: a fill, an answer or a root move.
- **In A minor.** A1 is 55 Hz, which sits well in the sub, and every preset stacks with every other. Some use Phrygian (B♭ in place of B) or harmonic minor (G♯ in place of G), and say so in their tags. **Filters > Transpose Drops to the Session's Key** moves them into the session's key as you drop them.
- **Drums on the General MIDI map:** kick 36, rim 37, snare 38, clap 39, closed hat 42, open hat 46, toms 41, 45 and 48, crash 49, ride 51, shaker 70. They play any drum plug-in or kit that follows it.
- **Bass one note at a time**, apart from an acid slide, which overlaps the next note by a hair so the synth glides.
- **Velocities carry the groove:**

  | Hit | Velocity |
  |---|---|
  | Accent | 110–127 |
  | Normal | 90–105 |
  | Weak hat | 60–80 |
  | Ghost | 25–50 |

- **Written at the style's usual tempo.** A sequence plays at the session's tempo wherever it goes, so this is only where it was written.
- **Original.** Each pattern is written to its style's conventions, not copied from a record, and named for what it is ("Iron Body Bass", "Rumble Roller"), never for a band or a song.
