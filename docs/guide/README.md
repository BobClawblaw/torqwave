# Torqwave user guide

How to use Torqwave, one job at a time. The [README](../../README.md) lists everything the app does; this guide shows you how to do it. The app carries this guide too: **Help > User Guide** (`F1`) opens it in a window of its own, with the pages listed down the left, links between them and Back and Forward.

Keys are written for the Mac. On Windows and Linux, read Ctrl for Cmd and Alt for Option, and Explorer (or your file manager) for Finder. Every key can be changed in Window > Keyboard Shortcuts..., and [SHORTCUTS.md](../SHORTCUTS.md) lists the ones Torqwave ships with.

## Pages

1. [Getting started](01-getting-started.md): installing, the first launch, the window, opening and saving.
2. [Editing audio](02-editing-audio.md): selecting, playing, cutting and pasting, fades, levels, markers, undo and saving.
3. [Spectral editing and repair](03-spectral-editing-and-repair.md): the spectrogram, selecting sounds by frequency, Heal, noise reduction and the repair effects.
4. [Effects and the rack](04-effects-and-the-rack.md): effects from the menu, presets, the live rack, plug-ins and recorded sequences.
5. [Loudness and mastering](05-loudness-and-mastering.md): measuring, matching a loudness target, delivery specs and finishing a master.
6. [Multitrack sessions](06-multitrack-sessions.md): tracks and clips, the tools, editing time, buses and sends, the mixer, automation, tempo and exporting.
7. [Instruments and MIDI](07-instruments-and-midi.md): instrument tracks, note clips, playing live, the Piano Roll, controller lanes, the Sequence Library and the Sequencer.
8. [Recording](08-recording.md): into a file, onto tracks, punch-in, monitoring, the metronome and loop takes.
9. [Video and surround](09-video-and-surround.md): editing to picture, writing sound back into video, 5.1 and 7.1 files and sessions.
10. [The Sound Library](10-sound-library.md): indexing, searching, auditioning and organising a sound collection.
11. [Projects and file types](11-projects-and-file-types.md): what each of Torqwave's files keeps, projects, and the audio formats.
12. [Customising and troubleshooting](12-customising-and-troubleshooting.md): settings, themes, keys, fixing common problems, and reporting one.
13. [Ask Torq 🐶 (experimental)](13-ask-torq.md): edits from a sentence, planned by a language model you run yourself.
